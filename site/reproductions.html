External reproduction

Reproductions: attempts, in the open

The top label on the evidence ladder — EXTERNALLY_REPRODUCED — is not yet attained, and this page is the place that fact becomes visible rather than implied. Every public reproduction attempt is listed here the moment its record lands: succeeded, diverged, or stopped early, with nothing filtered out. The board below is generated from the committed records, and the test suite fails if the page and the records drift apart — so the display cannot lag the evidence.

Current state

Zero external reproductions

EXTERNALLY_REPRODUCED not yet attained

No one outside this repository's authors has executed a released run and reported it. When that happens, the record is adjudicated against the published criteria and the label can land here — not before, and not by assertion.

Attempts are wanted regardless of outcome

A divergence is a contribution (it enters the minimize-then-classify counterexample path, never a tuning session); a walk that stopped early is friction data we act on. Only hiding an attempt would be a problem.

Reproduction is not physical validity

A reproduced result repeats the modeling choices it inherits. This label is orthogonal to calibration — landing it changes nothing about what the numbers mean (see Evidence).

Generated from the records

The board

One row per public attempt, rendered from experiments/reproductions/ by tools/reproduction_board.py. The label column reads UNKNOWN until an adjudication applies the verification procedure below — a report about oneself is not a judgment about oneself.

No reproduction attempts recorded yet. When one is reported on the funnel issue, it is transcribed into experiments/reproductions/ and appears here on the next board render — attempts are listed whether they succeed, diverge, or stop early, and nothing is filtered.

The verification procedure

What counts as independent

Four criteria, each judged once per attempt with a published note; a criterion that cannot be established is recorded as unmet — unknown is never rounded up. The full procedure, including what a report must contain and how a divergence is handled, is the reproduction policy in the repository.

Distinct hands

The reproducer is not among the people whose work is reproduced: no commits in this repository's history. An author's own re-run is at best VERIFIED — the rung below — and the record type refuses it here.

Distinct machine

Run on hardware and an OS that are not the authors' environment, from a fresh clone at a named public commit. Cross-platform byte-identity is the meaningful version of the determinism claim.

Released artifacts only

Nothing outside the public repository state: every run id, expected value, and command is derivable from the public tree (the reproduction cards pin the expected ids, so this is checkable by reading).

Public report

The attempt and its raw outputs are visible at a public URL, so any third party can audit the report itself — including this page's claims about it.

How to get on the board

Walk a card, report, done

  1. Pick a reproduction card (or the case-study card) and walk it on a clean clone.
  2. Post what happened — which card, which commit, your environment, the run ids you got versus the pinned ones — as a comment on the funnel issue.
  3. The report is transcribed into a committed record and appears here with label UNKNOWN; the independence criteria are then judged in the open, and the label moves (or does not) accordingly.

Friction-only reports (no re-executed runs) are equally welcome on the issue and need no record — they are how the cards get fixed.
