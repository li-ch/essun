Simulation paths

Four backends, one manifest, honest capability tables

The experiment manifest names the backend; capability gates reject any (model, topology, workload, observable) pairing the backend does not declare, before any execution. Everything below runs in-process or through a Docker-pinned image — and every row carries its evidence label.

The house rule, with machinery

When backends disagree: classify, do not tune

The cross-backend difference engine implements the house rule with machinery: exact and bounded obligations, an anti-tuning gate (registered tolerances only), a controlled-comparison gate (workload and topology hashes must match), and capability-gap not_comparable verdicts.

  • On the W1 smoke pair (runs 146ba565… / c6dc87eb…), the chain O-CCT gap of 7.404x (3248 ns vs 24048 ns per op) decomposes with zero residual into 2 declared modeling differences — a delay-placement difference (A-AS2-DELAY-POS) and a memory-bandwidth term (A-AS2-MEM-BW).
  • No parameter was tuned to make the curves match; the 2 counterexamples were classified and stored in the production counterexamples.jsonl, linked to the run.
  • A follow-up attribution probe on the third backend (a 0.976x ratio) is named in the ADR-0004 ratification record — named as follow-up work, not as a result.

Evidence label for the difference-engine story: IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED. See the Evidence page for the full reproducibility mechanism.
