The total nvlink bandwidth obtained by NCCL topo is not the actual total bandwidth#1054

Copy link

Description

PhdShi

opened on Nov 6, 2023

Issue body actions

Recently I was researching graph creation on an A100 8gpu server, and I found that the system->totalBw obtained at ncclTopoSearchInit was only 240GB/s. But I was able to observe a total nvlink bandwidth of 300GB/s with nvidia-smi nvlink -s. Further research shows that in the src/graph/topo.h file, an nvlink bandwidth is defined as 20GB/s instead of 25GB/s. Why is this?

The NVLink peak bandwidth and the real application-level bandwidth are different, since there is an overhead for NVLink packets. Roughly, the app-level bandwidth is 80% of the peak bandwidth.
