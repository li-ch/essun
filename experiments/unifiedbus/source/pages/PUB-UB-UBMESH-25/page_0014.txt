Support for Massive-Expert Models. Besides dense and regular MoE models, we notice that people are actively exploring MoE models containing massive experts (DeepSeek-AI et al., 2024b; DeepSeek-AI et al., 2024a). Such a design usually calls for large-scale, fine-grained all-to-all communication. UB-Mesh is able to efficiently support such massive-expert models via the multi-path and hierarchical all-to-all optimization (Sec. 5.1) and UB’s Load/Store based data transfer. The CCU can also efficiently offload all-to-all operations to save the usage of precious compute cores, as expected in (DeepSeek-AI et al., 2024b).

8. Conclusion

This paper proposes UB-Mesh, a novel datacenter network architecture designed for next-generation LLM training. UB-Mesh adopts an nD-FullMesh network topology to save the usage of switches and optical models, while adapts to the hierarchically localized traffic patterns of LLM workloads. We carefully consider the architecture, physical implementation and networking system optimization and propose several techniques to overcome various challenges. Compared to traditional Clos networks, UB-Mesh achieves similar LLM training performance while provides 2.042.04×\times higher system-level cost efficiency and 7.2% higher availability. UB-Mesh also achieves 95%+ linearity on several LLM training tasks.
