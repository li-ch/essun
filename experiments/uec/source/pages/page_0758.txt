Figure 7-5 - UE Switch Manageability Architecture 
The components of the UE switch manageability architecture can be described as follows. 
• Network switch is a component of the network fabric. Data ports are used for inter-server or 
inter-node fabric communications. Management port(s) of the switch platform connect to a 
separate and dedicated physical network infrastructure used for managing switches. 
• A remote management client manages the switch and directly communicates over 
management ports with the management agent on the switch. The management interface 
referenced here is the northbound interface from the NOS on the switch. 
• Management agent resides inside the NOS and exposes the northbound interface for 
management. Typically, the management agent is accessed over management ports. Remote 
management communication may happen over data ports. 
• SAI is the southbound interface on the switch. SAI is not visible to the remote management 
client. SAI is used for configuring and monitoring the switch ASIC. 
7.5.3 YANG Model Extensions for UE 
YANG (Yet Another Next Generation) is a data modeling language used to define the configuration and 
operational state data of network devices for management and automation. YANG provides a 
hierarchical, tree-based structure to model data, including configuration data, operational state, remote 
procedure calls (RPCs), and event notifications. YANG is protocol-independent and can be accessed by 
various protocols including OpenConfig and Netconf. YANG models can be serialized into various 
formats such as XML and JSON, facilitating automation and communication between network devices
