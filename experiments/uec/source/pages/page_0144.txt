UET verbs maintains a flexible access model to MRs for relative addressing. MR access restrictions can 
be: 
• MR is unrestricted on the PIDonFEP. Any peer may access it using any QP that shares the PD. 
• MR is restricted to a QP (i.e., RI restricted). Any peer may access it using only that QP.
