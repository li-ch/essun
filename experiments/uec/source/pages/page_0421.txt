The RODL header format when running over the UFH_16 header is illustrated in Figure 3-68. See 
Section 3.5.25.9 for a description of the RODL header mapped over the UFH_16 header. RODL also 
runs over UDP/IP/ETH or directly over UFH_32, as illustrated in Section 3.5.25.8. 
When using RODL over the UFH_16 header, the pds.flags field is reduced to 3 bits and the 
pds.psn_state is reduced to 24 bits. The header is compressed in the following ways: 
1. The 32-bit PSN is carried implicitly in the CRC once the PDC is established. 
a. This saves 32 bits on the wire by taking advantage of the in-order arrival of packets. 
b. During PDC establishment, the pds.psn field is carried in the header. 
i. RODL carries the 24 least significant bits of the full 32-bit PSN in the 
pds.psn_state.psn_lsb field. 
ii. All start PSN values have the upper 8 bits set to zero. 
2. The pds.pdc_info field used during PDC establishment is reduced to 2 bits such that 
pds.pdc_info = [rsvd, use_rsvd_pdc]. 
3. The pds.spdcid and pds.dpdcid field sizes are reduced to 12 bits. 
4. A 24-bit pds.psn_state field is defined. 
a. pds.psn_state carries the request PSN during PDC establishment (pds.flags.syn = 1). 
b. Once the PDC is established (flags.syn = 0), pds.psn_state carries 
{pds.psn_state.clear_psn_offset, pds.psn_state.rev_cack_psn_lsb}; each field is 12 bits. 
c. The PDC uses full 32-bit CLEAR_PSN and CACK_PSN, which are maintained on both sides 
of the PDC. The lsbs are carried in the packets. This limits the number of outstanding 
packets on a single RODL PDC. 
5. RODL does not support NSCC or packet trimming. 
a. RODL supports ACK and NACK. 
b. RODL does not support ACK_CC, ACK_CCX, and NACK_CCX. 
3.5.22.1 RODL Services 
RODL uses GoBackN retransmission such as ROD when retransmission is supported. RODL can operate 
without retransmission. If a packet is lost on a RODL PDC that does not support retransmission, the PDC 
is closed in error. Refer to Section 3.5.7.3. 
3.5.22.1.1 No PDS retransmission 
PDS is extended to support an option to exclude PDS retransmission on RODL PDCs. When 
retransmission is not supported, the loss of a single packet is fatal to the PDC and the PDC MUST be 
closed in error.
