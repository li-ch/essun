2.4.11 INC Transport  
INC leverages existing UET services and introduces a compact extension field to a semantic sublayer 
header to carry INC‑specific information. This extension field conveys fields such as the communicator 
ID, collective sequence number, and operation type. 
2.4.11.1 Semantic Operation 
INC uses UET_DEFERRABLE_SEND and UET_DEFERRABLE_RTR operations for data exchange. All 
necessary information for the collective operation is encoded in the 64-bit ses.inc_info field (see Figure 
2-24). 
The UET_HDR_REQUEST_STD (Figure 3-11 and Figure 3-12) is used for deferrable send operations. In 
UET_HDR_REQUEST_STD packets, the ses.match_bits field is replaced by the 64-bit ses.inc_info field. 
While the ses.match_bits fields in traditional send operations inform the destination where to place 
data and match messages, the ses.inc_info field informs the SEP how to process the data. 
Informative Text: 
The ses.inc_info field is overlayed upon the ses.match_bits field and it is difficult for an independent 
third party (e.g., a wireshark packet capture) to disambiguate the contents of these fields.  A switch 
understands how to process the ses.inc_info because it is aware of the {FA, JobID, PIDonFEP, RI} tuple 
in the request.
