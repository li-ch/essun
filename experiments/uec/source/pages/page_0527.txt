3.6.18.4.4 Signal-Accumulation Function Combinations  
Not all combinations of signals and accumulation functions are useful. This specification defines a 
mandatory subset to ensure implementations can specialize for a minimal, interoperable baseline. Other 
combinations may be provided. An implementation MAY implement accumulation functions for any 
signal that it finds useful.
