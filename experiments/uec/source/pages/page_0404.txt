3.5.20.2.3 Multi-Packet Send Sequence—Non-Guaranteed Delivery 
Send and write sequences are the same on best-effort and lossless networks. The standard RUD Send 
sequence for a multi-packet message is shown in Figure 3-99. The sequence in Figure 3-99proceeds as 
follows: 
1. The RUD send sequence for multi-packet messages is the same as the RUD send sequence for a 
single-packet message. 
a. The single-packet sequence is repeated for each packet of the multi-packet message. 
b. Packets may arrive out of order at the destination. 
c. Acknowledgements may be cumulative—the figure shows an ACK every two packets; 
however, ACK generation events vary when cumulative ACKs are used; see Section 
3.5.12.2.
