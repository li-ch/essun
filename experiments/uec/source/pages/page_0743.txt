For links that use 1/(1+D) precoding (available in CR and KR PHY types), errors occur in pairs. 
Consequently, the estimate of Equation 7 is inadequate in the following specific case: 100 Gb/s Ethernet 
with the IEEE Std 802.3-2022 clause 91 RS-FEC (where interleaving is not used) and precoding enabled. 
This case has a much higher UCR for the same SER. For links requiring high MTBPE, it is RECOMMENDED 
to use IEEE Std 802.3-2022 clause 161 RS-FEC_int instead.
