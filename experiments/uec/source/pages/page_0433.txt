3.5.25.8 UET RODL - General 
Figure 3-119 shows UET RODL over the UFH_32 header. UET RODL also runs over a traditional Ethernet 
header using the same format.  The RODL header, beginning at header start byte 16 in Figure 3-119, 
remains 12 bytes whether carried over Ethernet or over UDP/IP/Ethernet, consistent with the behavior 
of RUD and ROD. The pds.entropy field is always immediately after the UET EtherType. 
3.5.25.9 UET RODL, ACK, NACK, and CP over the UFH_16 Header 
Figure 3-120 illustrates UET over the UFH_16 header, which is optimized for a single tier of switching. 
Only PDS types RODL, ACK, NACK, and control packets are defined for the UFH_16 header. The 
illustration shows how these PDS headers are mapped into available bytes in the unified forwarding 
header with remaining fields following the EtherType.  
TSS is not supported with the UFH_16 header.
