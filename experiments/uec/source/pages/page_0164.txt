2.4.12 INC Data Plane Overview 
This section specifies the INC data plane protocol. The IM controls the initial creation phase of the INC 
communicator. Once the INC communicator is created, collective operations may be issued repeatedly 
without additional setup. The IM also controls the destruction of the INC communicator. Refer to 
Section 2.4.8 for an overview of the command flows. 
2.4.12.1 Data Plane Sequences 
Once the communicator is active, with all PDCs established, collective operations can be performed. 
Figure 2-25 shows an example of executing a CCL allreduce operation. Each packet carries an integer 
number of elements and sufficient information for the SEP to perform the operation autonomously. The 
state required at the SEP is primarily the INC communicator membership and root FEP for reduce 
operations. 
TFC may be used if the switch has limited buffering and needs to limit the amount of data each member 
FEP sends. In some deployments, CBFC may be adequate and TFC is not necessary. 
The initialization phase is not shown in Figure 2-25. 
In an allreduce operation, every member FEP receives output data. Other collective operations, such as 
reduce, may produce output data only for a designated root FEP. In those cases, the acknowledgement 
from the SEP that all input data has been received indicates completion of the collective operation. 
Figure 2-26 shows the flow when a member FEP receive buffer is not available. This condition causes all 
traffic on the INC communicator to pause.
