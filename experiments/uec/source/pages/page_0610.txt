4.2.4.1.7 Locator Metadata (csig.lm) 
Locator metadata is a 6-bit field in the compact tag and a 15-bit field in the wide tag. It identifies the 
location within the network and/or attributes of the switch where the signal value in a CSIG tag was 
updated. When a switch updates the signal value in a packet’s tag, it MUST also update the locator 
metadata in the tag. 
Locator metadata is updated by the bottleneck hop (i.e., the hop updating the signal value as part of the 
CSIG-Compare-and-Update() operation). Locator metadata may provide deployment-specific 
information for deployment-specific differentiation or vendor extensions (e.g., vendor-specific 
enhancements to UE CC algorithms). Locator metadata may provide additional hints to improve UE 
congestion management, path selection, or just provide visibility of the bottleneck hop as telemetry 
data.
