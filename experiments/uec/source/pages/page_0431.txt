3.5.25.7 UET over UFH_32 Header 
Figure 3-118 illustrates UET over the UFH_32 header. All PDS types can be used over the UFH_32 header 
with the following differences noted: 
• A 16-bit version of the entropy header is carried after the EtherType. The TSS or PDS header 
starts after the entropy field with type as the first byte. 
o The 2-byte entropy header is always present after the EtherType field. 
o The TSS or PDS header directly follows the entropy field. 
• All PDS types will add pad bytes such that the UET CRC can be located directly before the 
Ethernet FCS—see Section 3.5.26. 
This applies to all PDS packet types: RUD, ROD, RODL, RUDI, UUD, ACK, NACK, and control packets.
