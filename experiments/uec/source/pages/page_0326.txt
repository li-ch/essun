3.5.9.2 PDS Manager 
Within PDS, some functions are general and others are specific to individual PDCs. Examples of general 
functions include the allocation of PDCs, the handling of error events that are not associated with a 
specific PDC, the assignment of SES packets to PDCs, etc. These services are provided by the PDS 
manager state machine. Figure 3-60 illustrates the services within the PDS manager.
