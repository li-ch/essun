Congestion Signaling (CSIG): Congestion Signaling (CSIG) is a lightweight in-band telemetry mechanism 
designed to meet the demands of modern AI, HPC, and high-speed datacenter networks, where 
traditional congestion signals such as ECN, packet loss, and RTT are too slow and coarse-grained. By 
embedding a small fixed-size tag in packets, CSIG collects real-time, per-path congestion information—
such as available bandwidth, capacity, delay, and queue buildup—directly from network switches, 
enabling faster and more accurate congestion control, improved flow completion times, better network 
utilization, and enhanced visibility for troubleshooting, monitoring, and traffic engineering, all with 
minimal overhead and compatibility with encrypted traffic.

features to the Link Layer. Figure 1-17 shows the areas of focus for the UE link layer specifications with
