Receivers MAY dynamically determine Rcv_Cwnd_Pend depending on the degree of destination 
congestion. The specifics of how the Rcv_Cwnd_Pend is calculated is implementation dependent. 
The field may be set to 0 if it is not supported by the receiver. 
The source responds to non-zero values of Rcv_Cwnd_Pend by modifying the congestion control 
response. This is detailed in apply_cwnd_penalty() in the NSCC pseudocode.
