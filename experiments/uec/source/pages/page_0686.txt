The UE LLDP database is defined under a UE organizationally defined YANG module. The information 
within the UE LLDP database is used both by the network management system to discover the 
capabilities and topology of the network and by the link partners to negotiate the operation of optional 
UE link features. UE feature negotiation is performed by the local management procedures of each 
individual feature using the local and remote UE LLDP database information. 
The format of an LLDP frame is illustrated in Figure 5-18. The LLDPDU contains a series of TLVs 
formatted as described in [1] . These include three mandatory TLVs—Chassis ID, Port ID, and Time To 
Live — followed by a series of optional TLVs. The UE organizationally specific LLDP TLVs are included 
among the optional TLVs.
