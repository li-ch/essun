The capabilities node holds information regarding switch capabilities. The IM uses these capabilities to 
determine if an INC switch is eligible to be used for INC offload for a particular communicator. The IM 
reads the switch capabilities and performs the following: 
• The IM ensures that at least one of the protocol types included in the available-protocol-types 
node is supported by all the other switches used by the communicator. 
• The IM verifies that the set of capabilities provided by the switch matches the set of capabilities 
required by the ranks. The capabilities are listed in Table 2-54. 
• The IM verifies that the resources listed in Table 2-55 are available on the switch.
