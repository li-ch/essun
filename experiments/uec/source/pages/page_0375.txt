3.5.14 Transmit Scheduling  
PDS should avoid starving any PDCs sharing a traffic class for an extended period of time while servicing 
other PDCs. PDS may provide hierarchical scheduling where some PDCs are given higher priority than 
other PDCs in the same traffic class. This applies to PDS requests, CPs, and acknowledgements. 
Higher priority SHOULD be done as weighted fair queuing (WFQ) rather than strict priority (SP), as SP 
scheduling may lead to blocking or starvation.
