5.4.3 UFH Definition 
The UFH is defined as an overlay on the 12-byte Ethernet MAC header with special encodings in the 
MAC DA and MAC SA fields. Fields identified as essential to UFH are overlaid on this 12-byte base 
header, and UFH defines two sub-variants to cover different needs. The Ethernet rule of inserting an 
EtherType field at offset 12 from the start of frame is followed to maintain backward compatibility and 
to allow UFH and standard Ethernet + IP frames to coexist on a link. This EtherType identifies the next 
header in the stack and is not essential for UFH itself.
