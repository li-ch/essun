2.4.6 Operator Ordering Modes 
INC implementations MUST support at least one of the following operator ordering modes when 
configured through the IM: 
• No ordering: No guarantee of the reduction order. 
• Consistent ordering in the same communicator: For the same communicator, the switch always 
applies the same order of reduction operations. 
• Consistent ordering across different communicators: For different communicators with 
identical attributes in the same job, the switch always applies the same order of reduction 
operations. 
• Consistent ordering across different communicators and jobs: For different jobs with identical 
node allocations and different communicators with identical attributes in those jobs, the switch 
always applies the same order of reduction operations. 
• Natural ordering: The switch reduces in the order of ranks beginning from 0 to p-1. For example  
p=4: sum(sum(sum(0,1),2),3); note that this mode limits parallelism and may lead to lower 
performance. 
2.4.7 Operator Rounding Modes 
INC implementations MUST support the following floating-point rounding modes: 
• Round to nearest, ties to even. 
INC implementations MAY support any of the following floating-point rounding modes: 
• Round to nearest, ties away from zero.
