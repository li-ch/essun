3.5.22 Reliable Ordered Delivery for Local Networks 
A RODL PDC is a reliable PDC established between a pair of FEPs that guarantees packets are delivered 
at most once. RODL is a variation of ROD that is optimized for scale-up networks. RODL is different from 
ROD in the following notable ways: 
1. RODL runs over a bidirectional PDC. 
a. Targets may initiate send, write, or atomic requests on the PDC—see Section 3.5.22.1.2.
