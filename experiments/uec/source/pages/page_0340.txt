3.5.10.8 ACK_CC   
The ACK with congestion control format is used to carry information for the congestion management 
sublayer. When NSCC is used, the ACK_CC and/or ACK_CCX are used. 
The ACK_CC format is shown in Figure 3-71. The ACK_CCX fields are described in Table 3-45.

3.5.10.9 ACK_CCX 
The extended congestion control ACK format is one of the tools used to enable flexibility in the 
congestion management schemes as those schemes evolve in the future. When NSCC is used, at least 
one of ACK_CC or ACK_CCX is used.
