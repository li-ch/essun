Note: 
1. CBFC best-effort packets (types A and C) never update any CBFC counters.  
2. CBFC lossless counters are updated when the packet is first transmitted before the LLR re-transmission is 
triggered, and therefore must not be updated again during replay.  
3. The combination of LLR-ineligible and CBFC Lossless is not allowed. 
 
CC_Update packets must always be marked as LLR-eligible so that they carry an LLR sequence number 
and can be discarded if previous CBFC lossless packets (type D) were discarded just prior to the 
CC_Update packet arrival. In this case, the CC_Update packet itself triggers the start of the LLR discard 
window. The three allowed types of data packets in Table 5-26, (A, C, and D) are used in Figure 5-13 and 
Figure 5-14 to illustrate two examples of the combined LLR and CBFC handling during an LLR re-
transmission event. In Figure 5-13, the CC_Update packet is received after the LLR-discard window has 
already been triggered. In Figure 5-14, the CC_Update packet is received as the first “out of sequence” 
LLR-eligible packet. In both cases, the CC_Update packet is initially discarded by the receiver and it is 
later replayed.
