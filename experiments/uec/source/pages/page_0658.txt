5.2.5.6.1 Packet Transmission Credit Check 
To select a packet from a lossless VC for transmission and to make counter updates, the number of 
credits required for the packet must first be computed: 
pktSize = size of the MAC frame in bytes 
pktCredits = roundup((pktSize + PktOvhd) ÷ CreditSize) 
 
Informative Text: 
The size of a MAC frame includes the bytes beginning with the MAC DA and ending with the FCS.
