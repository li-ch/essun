5.4.12 UFH Efficiency Calculations 
Packet efficiency for different header permutations is calculated using the equation below: 
𝐸 = 𝑃𝑎𝑦𝑙𝑜𝑎𝑑 𝑆𝑖𝑧𝑒𝑃𝑎𝑦𝑙𝑜𝑎𝑑 𝑆𝑖𝑧𝑒 + 𝐻𝑒𝑎𝑑𝑒𝑟 𝑂𝑣𝑒𝑟ℎ𝑒𝑎𝑑  
The header overhead adds all L2, L3, and L4 headers, with IPG and preamble ignored for simplicity. 
Opaque fields are not included in the overhead. A common transport size is assumed for all, and the 
resulting efficiency numbers in tabular and chart form are as below. For small payloads and compressed 
headers, the minimum Ethernet frame size is 64 B.
