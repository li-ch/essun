3.5.15.1 Trimming 
Packet trimming is used by UET to provide an explicit indication of packet loss and to enhance 
congestion control performance. If the packet is validated using the UET CRC or authenticated using TSS 
and the packet was not actually trimmed because it is shorter than MIN_TRIM_SIZE, the packet can be 
accepted for normal processing.
