When trimming is enabled, the RECOMMENDED default settings are:

In the absence of trimming, the UET congestion management algorithm supports widely deployed 
shared switch buffer architectures. Although the algorithm is capable of handling highly variable 
latencies caused by the shared buffer, it is recommended to bound the tail drop threshold. The 
RECOMMENDED drop threshold for a given queue should be at least 2 * Plane_BDP and at most 
5 * Plane_BDP. 
Rather than using strict priority, a deployment MAY choose to use the same priority for queue_med and 
queue_low, but instead use weighted round robin or a similar scheduling scheme to ensure that when 
the trim rate is high, queue_med gets a large share of the bandwidth. In such cases a reasonable default 
is for queue_med to be weighted to receive 75% of the capacity when competing with queue_low. 
3.6.17.1 CSIG Configuration 
The overall bucket boundaries for the max(Delay) signal quantization SHOULD be configured based on 
the trim or drop threshold recommendations, regardless of whether the compact or wide CSIG header 
format is used. 
The recommended queue_low.trim_threshold, queue_med.drop_threshold, and 
queue_high.drop_threshold are specified as factors of Plane_BDP, which is derived from the link speed 
and config_base_rtt. This information is used to derive expected maximum packet queueing times per 
trim or drop threshold, which equates Plane_BDP to config_base_rtt as follows: 
Plane_BDP = min(sender.linkspeed, receiver.linkspeed) * config_base_rtt  
 
However, this equation assumes that only a single queue is active per port. To account for scenarios 
where other, higher-priority queues are active and may increase maximum queueing times, it is 
RECOMMENDED to apply a 2-times multiplier to the threshold-derived maximum packet queueing time. 
Informative Text: 
These queue threshold settings are based on simulated results to date. Lower drop thresholds may 
lead to additional loss of high-priority traffic. Additional tuning of deployed implementations may be 
required.
