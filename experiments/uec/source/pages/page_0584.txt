3.7.12 Statistics, Parameters, and Events  
Table 3-137 describes the counters and statistics that MUST be supported. Counters MUST initialize to 
zero and wrap when their bit width is exceeded. These counters MUST be provided to the management 
entity within the FEP. These are modeled after the Ethernet MIB counters [44]. All counters MUST be 64 
bits when presented to the SDME.
