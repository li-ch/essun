3.6.18.10 eBPF Compliance 
Implementations MAY advertise PCM eBPF compliance. This allows implementations to formally verify 
loaded algorithms for safety and security. Algorithms written to comply with this capability MUST 
adhere to the following constraints: 
• Only bounded loops 
• No recursion 
• All pointer accesses must be expressed so they can be verified for range and access validation 
• Limit stack size to a maximum of 512 B 
• Limit call depth to a maximum depth of 8 calls 
• Limit tail-call depth to a maximum of 32 tail-calls
