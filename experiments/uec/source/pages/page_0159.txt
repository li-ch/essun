2.4.8 INC Libfabric  
The creation of a communicator is initiated by the ranks calling fi_join_collective() with matching 
arguments. Figure 2-22 shows the sequence of exchanges between the IM, an exemplary rank, and an 
INC switch during INC communicator creation. 
After all ranks send a CommunicatorJoinRequest, the IM allocates fabric resources for the operation of 
the INC communicator, subject to immediate availability and, optionally, any vendor-specific policies. 
The IM configures each switch individually and sends a CommunicatorJoinReply to the communicator 
ranks with the information required to communicate with their adjacent SEPs. 
The rank then allocates an initiator PDS and performs a UET_INC_BARRIER collective, which forces the 
establishment of both the initiator and target PDCs.
