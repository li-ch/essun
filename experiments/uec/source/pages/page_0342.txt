• 4 => Close Command 
o Initiator indicating the PDC is being closed 
• 5 => Close Request 
o Target request to initiator to close the PDC 
• 6 => Probe 
o Source to destination to request PDS ACK 
• 7 => Credit 
o Destination to source carrying congestion

o Source to destination requesting credit
