3.5.8.4 PDC Lifetime 
The lifetime of a PDC depends on the use case of the network. An implementation may choose to 
provide configuration parameters to control PDC lifetime, but these are not specified here. A PDC may 
exist for a very brief time—as short as the time to send a single packet and receive the associated 
acknowledgement plus one RTT. This could occur when there is pressure on the number of PDCs 
supported by an implementation. Alternatively, a PDC may exist for an extended period, as long as the 
life of an application (or more).
