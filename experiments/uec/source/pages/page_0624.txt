4.2.9.3 max(Delay) 
When a source FEP sends a packet with a CSIG tag for signal type max(Delay), and that packet traverses 
a path through the UE fabric, that packet’s CSIG tag records, among all switches along the packet’s path, 
the maximum service delay in nanoseconds incurred by the packet at any one switch. Both the 
unquantized and quantized delay values are represented as integers. 
As each switch computes its own intra-switch delay using only switch-local timestamps, there is no need 
for clock synchronization across switches in support of these measurements. 
4.2.9.3.1 Per-Hop Delay Measurement Method 
In a CSIG-capable fabric, at each hop along a path, the switch pipeline measures per-packet delay.
