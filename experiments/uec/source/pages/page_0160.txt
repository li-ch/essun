The communication between the libfabric provider and the IM is specified in Section 2.4.15. The 
communication between the IM and the switch is specified in Section 2.4.17. 
After all collective operations of the communicator have completed, the ranks request that the IM tear 
down the INC communicator. Figure 2-23 depicts the sequence of steps taken by the IM, a rank, and an 
INC switch. 
The IM first waits for all ranks to produce a CommunicatorLeaveRequest to allow for all collectives to 
complete. Afterward, it starts independent procedures at both the rank and the switch. 
The rank receives a CommunicatorLeaveReply, whereupon the initiator PDC is closed. 
The INC switch is directed to terminate the INC communicator. Following this, the switch adjusts its 
internal configuration, disabling INC offload for the communicator, and closes the SEP’s initiator PDC. 
The libfabric provider MAY implement a mechanism to release target PDCs that the switch failed to 
close. Such a mechanism is beyond the scope of this document. 
The IM can also tear down an INC communicator for any of the following reasons: 
• A rank signals an error. 
• Conditions are detected that preclude continued INC communicator operation (e.g., link 
failures). 
• Administrative action or policy.
