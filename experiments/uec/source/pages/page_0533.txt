3.6.18.5.3 Trigger Evaluation 
Triggers are logically evaluated as follows: 
• When a packet is received, all armed triggers associated with accumulators tracking uet_rx 
signals are evaluated 
• When a packet is transmitted, all armed triggers associated with accumulators tracking uet_tx 
signals are evaluated 
• When a time event occurs, all armed triggers associated with accumulators tracking uet_time 
signals are evaluated 
The algorithm MUST be invoked exactly once to deliver all fired (non-deferred) triggers; piecemeal 
delivery is not allowed. Algorithms determine the set of fired triggers by examining the trigger bitmask 
delivered with every invocation. 
Multiple deferred invocation triggers are reconciled as follows:  
• A per-CCC deadline variable maintains the minimum deadline to the next invocation.  
• For every evaluated trigger, if the trigger has a non-zero invocation deadline, the per-CCC 
deadline is updated as follows: 
CCC.invoke_deadline = min(CCC.invoke_deadline,now() +     
trigger.invoke_deadline) 
3.6.18.5.4 Trigger Updates 
On successful application of a trigger update, the PCM subsystem MUST reset all trigger working states. 
After resetting, the trigger MUST be immediately eligible for evaluation and firing under the new 
configuration. All trigger reference value fidelities match those of the associated signal.
